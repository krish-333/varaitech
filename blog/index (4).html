Why Electronics Distributors' Stock Never Matches Tally, and How to Fix It

DistributionBy VARAI Technologies2 Nov 20263 min read

An electronics or electrical distributor can carry thousands of SKUs, many of them similar in name, size and rating. Goods come in from many suppliers, go out in small and large lots, and are billed in Tally or a similar tool. In theory the numbers should agree. In practice they rarely do.

Small gaps add up until physical stock, Tally and purchase orders tell three different stories. These are the usual causes.

1. Stock is counted in one place and billed in another

The godown team counts goods, the billing team raises invoices, and someone reconciles the two by hand. Any step that depends on a person remembering to update a sheet will eventually be missed.

2. Look-alike SKUs get mixed up

When two items differ only by a rating or a brand, a wrong entry is easy. The error stays hidden until a customer asks for the item and it is not where the system says it is.

3. Slow movers hide while fast movers run out

Without a clear view of what sells and what sits, cash gets tied up in dead stock while popular items are out. Buying decisions turn into guesswork.

4. Purchase orders and follow-ups live in calls and chats

Which order is pending with which supplier, and when it is due, often depends on one person. Delays are found only when a customer is already waiting.

What a better system looks like

One stock record updates as goods are received, billed and returned, with history at SKU level. Low-stock alerts and dead-stock reports guide buying. Purchase orders are tracked from request to delivery, so everyone sees what is coming.

Importantly, it can read from your existing accounts instead of replacing them. Your team keeps using Tally for accounting, and the stock system keeps the physical side honest.

What to do about it

Begin with your top-moving SKUs and the receiving and billing flow. Once those numbers match, extend to the rest of the range and to purchase orders.
