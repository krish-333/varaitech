Inventory Control for Auto Parts Dealers With Thousands of SKUs

RetailBy VARAI Technologies2 Nov 20263 min read

Auto parts dealers sell a huge range of small items, from filters to brake pads to bearings, across many makes and models. Counters are busy all day, and that is exactly where mismatches and pilferage begin.

Here is where manual stock handling usually costs dealers the most.

1. Parts are hard to find

When staff rely on memory or searching shelves, a sale can be lost simply because nobody can find the part, even if it is in stock.

2. Fast movers run out, slow movers tie up cash

Without a clear view of what sells, orders to suppliers follow habit. The result is empty shelves on popular parts and dead stock on the rest.

3. Differences appear only at the yearly count

Small leaks from miscounts, missed entries or theft stay invisible for months. By the time they show up, it is hard to trace the cause.

4. Pricing and suppliers are scattered

Rates by supplier and by brand sit in books and phone chats, so choosing the best source for a part takes time.

What a better system looks like

A searchable stock list by part number, vehicle and rack location. Reports that separate fast, slow and dead stock, so reordering follows sales. Regular cycle counts that flag differences early, not once a year.

Counter staff get an answer in seconds, and the owner gets a clear view without standing at the shop all day.

What to do about it

Start with your top selling parts and a simple location scheme. Once counts match, add reorder alerts and supplier rates.
