Stop Losing Customer Orders in WhatsApp: Order Tracking for Job-Work Units

OperationsBy VARAI Technologies2 Nov 20263 min read

Job-work and contract manufacturing orders often arrive as a photo of a drawing, a voice note or a quick call. The details go on a slip of paper or into a chat, and that is where things start to slip.

These are the most common problems we see in job-work units.

1. The shop floor works from old information

Customers change quantities, dates or specs on WhatsApp, and the update does not always reach the people doing the job. Rework follows.

2. Nobody sees the full list of open jobs

Without one place that shows what is in progress, ready and waiting, dispatch gets delayed and priorities change by who shouted last.

3. Dispatch happens, invoicing does not

Goods leave, and the invoice is raised late or forgotten. Cash flow suffers for work that is already done.

4. The owner is the only tracking system

Status questions from customers all go to one person, who has to chase the floor and answer. It does not scale.

What a better system looks like

Every order is captured once with quantity, specs and due date, visible to everyone who needs it. A live job board shows what is in progress, ready and dispatched. Reminders link dispatch to invoicing so finished work always gets billed.

Customers get quick answers, and the owner is no longer the status desk.

What to do about it

Start with capturing orders in one place. Once the board is trusted, add dispatch and invoice reminders.
