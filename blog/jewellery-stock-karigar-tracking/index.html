Jewellery Stock and Karigar Tracking: Why Registers and Excel Fall Short

JewelleryBy VARAI Technologies2 Nov 20263 min read

Few businesses handle stock as valuable and as mobile as a jewellery business. Gold, silver and stones move between the counter, the safe, the workshop and karigars every day. Most jewellers manage this with registers and Excel sheets, and for a long time it works, because the owner knows the business closely.

The trouble starts when volume grows, a second person handles stock, or an audit needs answers fast. Here is where manual tracking begins to cost jewellers real time and money.

1. Karigar issue and return is reconciled late

Metal issued to a karigar is noted in a register, and the finished piece comes back days or weeks later. Wastage, purity and weight differences are worked out by hand, often at the end of the month. By then nobody remembers why a difference appeared.

2. Stock lives in too many sheets

Counter stock, safe stock, workshop stock and old stock are often tracked separately, by weight, purity or design. When a customer asks for a particular design, someone has to check several places, and the answer is not always right.

3. Month-end takes days

Reconciling registers, sheets and bills is slow, and it still leaves differences that are hard to explain. Time that belongs to customers and sourcing goes into checking numbers.

4. The owner cannot see the full picture

Pending karigar jobs, slow-moving designs and outstanding payments sit in different notebooks. Decisions about what to make, buy or discount are based on memory rather than data.

What a better system looks like

Every issue, return and wastage entry is recorded once, with weight and purity, against a karigar and a job. Stock is visible by category, location and design in one place, and it updates as items are sold or moved. Pending jobs, slow movers and payments show up in simple views the owner can open any time.

The key is that the system follows how your shop already works. Your terms, your categories and your way of handling karigars stay the same, and the software takes over the writing, adding and checking.

What to do about it

Start with one flow, usually karigar issue and return, because it carries the highest value and the most risk. Prove the time saved, then extend to stock and payments.
